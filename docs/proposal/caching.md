# §3.1 Caching strategy — draft

*Draft for the team. Every sentence is checked against the code listed below it; change the code and this changes.*

Nothing that decides money is served from a cache that could say yes when the answer is no. Balances are never stored: each read is `SUM(amount)` over the account's ledger entries (ADR 0009). The one copy on the trade path is the ledger's list of suspended traders and published markets, refreshed from auth and market_service about once a second, so a trade calls neither service (ADR 0030). It is not a correctness cache, because a stale copy can only refuse: past five seconds old, every trade is refused `503` until it refreshes. Its age limit decides how soon trading stops when auth or market_service stops answering, never whether a wrong trade goes through. The cost is that a suspension or an early close takes about a second to reach trading. The leaderboard is a snapshot rebuilt every 60 seconds and marked `stale` past two intervals (ADR 0021). A market's `b` is copied once, because a published market's terms are frozen, and a market_service 404 is remembered for 10 seconds. Redis (managed Valkey in production) carries the live-price bus and auth's login-attempt counters, which fail open (ADR 0027). No API route sets HTTP cache headers; the gateway tells browsers to keep fingerprinted frontend assets for a year and to revalidate `index.html`.

**Checked against**

- Balances are derived. The sum is at `backend/ledger_service/service/accounts.py:93-95`, and `backend/ledger_service/model/entities.py:200-201` records that the account has no balance column.
- The ledger's local copy. Two refreshers per ledger replica, started at `backend/ledger_service/main.py:38-80`, fetch auth's `GET /internal/users/suspended` and market_service's `GET /internal/markets/published` (`backend/ledger_service/service/local_copy.py:321-388`). The interval and the age limit are `local_copy_refresh_seconds` = 1.0 and `local_copy_max_age_seconds` = 5.0 (`backend/ledger_service/core/config.py:81-82`). A copy past its age, or never loaded, raises `AuthUnavailable` or `MarketTermsUnavailable`, both `503` (`local_copy.py:460-511`). Why the age limit is a refusal setting and not a correctness one, and the one-second window: ADR 0030, "The TTL is a refusal parameter, and a stale copy never says yes" and "The one window it opens is about one second". The deciding close check still runs under the book lock on Postgres's clock (`backend/ledger_service/service/books.py`, `refuse_if_closed_under_lock`). The ledger's `/ready` reports both copies' freshness (`backend/ledger_service/main.py:177-187`).
- The leaderboard snapshot. The 60-second default and the two-interval staleness are at `backend/ledger_service/core/config.py:66-74`. Generations are stored at `backend/ledger_service/model/entities.py:679` (`LeaderboardGeneration`). The refresh loop is `backend/ledger_service/service/leaderboard_refresher.py`.
- The book's copy of the terms is `MarketBook` at `backend/ledger_service/model/entities.py:407-432` ("A snapshot, not a live read").
- The 404 memory is set up at `backend/ledger_service/service/market_terms.py:38-47` and read at `:319`. It is implemented in `backend/ledger_service/core/not_found_cache.py`, per process, and the decision is DECISIONS.md "A market_service 404 is remembered for ten seconds, per process".
- Redis. The ledger publishes in `backend/ledger_service/service/bus.py:31-62` after the trade commits, and the realtime service subscribes in `backend/realtime_service/service/bus.py`. Auth's client is `backend/auth_service/core/cache.py`; the limiter counts with `INCR` and `EXPIRE NX` at `backend/auth_service/service/attempt_limits.py:87-101`, and lets the request through when Redis fails (ADR 0027, "Fail open"). Production gives auth the same `REDIS_URL` as the ledger and realtime (`deploy/production/compose.yml:101,129,149`). Locally, persistence is off at `docker-compose.yml:275` (`--save "" --appendonly no`). In production the bus is `infra/terraform/databases.tf:27-37`, which sets no persistence option, so the paragraph claims none.
- No HTTP caching on the API. A grep for `Cache-Control|ETag|no-store` over `backend/` (outside tests) finds nothing; the auth cookie's `max_age` (`backend/auth_service/controller/transport.py:53,62`) is the cookie's lifetime. The production gateway's asset headers are at `deploy/production/gateway/default.conf.template:88-89,99`. The `lru_cache` uses build one object per process, each service's settings and its tracer provider (`backend/*/core/config.py`, `backend/*/core/observability.py`), not data.
- No frontend query cache. The dependencies at `frontend/package.json:19-25` are `axios`, `react`, `react-dom`, `react-router-dom` and `recharts`. The 30-second fallback poll, for markets the socket is not carrying, is `PRICE_REFRESH_MS` at `frontend/src/hooks/usePricesForMarkets.ts:60`.
- The refusals of a yes-saying cache: ADR 0017 and ADR 0022 rejected caching the per-trade answers, and ADR 0030 records why its copy is not that cache ("Context", quoting ADR 0022's reversal note).
